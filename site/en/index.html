EDC Radio

A Windows radio for the Easy Delivery Co. soundtrack. Seven stations on the in-game frequencies, a clock-driven broadcast, static between stations and an overlay on top of the game.

download EdcRadio.exe source on GitHub

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · 66 MB · .NET inside the exe

v1.0.1

Stations 88.1–107.9

The frequencies and track order match the radio in the cab. A station comes in within 0.4 MHz, and between stations there is static with noise dots.

88.1your radio, files from the game's CustomRadio folder
91.1EasyCo radio loops Rainless
99.1EZ News airs in the cab only, here you get a ticker
99.9lo-fi, 9 Sohaoying tracks in 10 slots
101.7drum and bass, 10 pr0te tracks
104.9rally, 6 Sohaoying tracks
107.9after hours, Shut Down and Rainless (Hum)

The broadcast counts from 1 January 2025 UTC. A song is usually already playing when you tune in, and two players on two computers stay within a second of each other.

Tape, scan, sleep

  • Tape plays the album in order. Side A is the 22 tracks of disc 1, side B the 6 tracks of disc 2.
  • Scan moves to the next station every 6 seconds.
  • Sleep runs 15, 30 or 60 minutes and fades out over 10 seconds.
  • Keys 1 to 7 pick the stations, like preset buttons on a car radio.

Overlay on a game

Ctrl+Alt+O turns the window into a strip above all windows. The Ctrl+Alt keys work while a game has focus.

  • The black behind the lines is 0, 30, 60 or 100 % opaque, and the glow stays.
  • Mini mode is 320 by 64 pixels with the frequency, station, track and time.
  • Clicks pass through the strip into the game.
  • A tray icon with a menu and a settings window laid out like the game's settings.

The scene

The truck drives along a night road while music plays, at 10 to 34 pixels a second depending on the station. From 18:00 to 7:00 the headlights are on and the moon and stars are out. The cat at the radio tower nods to the beat, and every 40 to 120 seconds the snow shifts between a few flakes and a blizzard.

Where the music comes from

The player contains no music and does not search your disk for it. There are three sources:

  • the Steam soundtrack, app 3994280, found on its own when installed;
  • a folder you pick with the "folder" button;
  • the game's CustomRadio folder for station 88.1.

Without music the window offers to pick a folder or to open the soundtrack page on Steam.

The soundtrack on Steam

If something breaks

  • A track that does not open is skipped, and its name shows in the status line.
  • On a crash the player saves its settings and writes a report to %APPDATA%\EdcRadio\crash.log.
  • A damaged settings.json is kept next to it as settings.json.bad, and the player starts with default settings.

Report a bug: the form asks for the version, Windows and where the music comes from. If there is a crash.log, attach it.

What it does not do

  • It contains no music and no game graphics. It reads the fonts and icons read-only from a computer where the game is installed.
  • It changes nothing in the game or Steam files.
  • The overlay does not show over games in exclusive fullscreen. It works with windowed and borderless games.
  • The build is not signed, so SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher on the first run.
  • It is not an official program. The game's author and the composers have nothing to do with it.
