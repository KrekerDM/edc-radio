EDC Radio

The Easy Delivery Co. truck radio for Windows. Seven stations, a clock-driven broadcast, static between stations and an overlay on top of the game. The music comes from Steam or a folder you pick.

download EdcRadio.exe source on GitHub

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · 66 MB · .NET inside the exe

The build is not signed, so SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher on the first run. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

v1.1.0

Stations 88.1–107.9 MHz

Six stations sit on the frequencies of the radio in the cab, with the same track order. The seventh, 107.9, is the player's own: it plays two tracks that the game's radio leaves out. A station comes in within 0.4 MHz, and between stations there is static with noise dots on the screen.

88.1your radio, files you put in the game's CustomRadio folder
91.1EasyCo radio loops Rainless
99.1EZ News airs in the cab only, so here you get static and a ticker
99.9lo-fi, 9 Sohaoying tracks in 10 slots, Easily Delivered or Whatever plays twice
101.7drum and bass, 10 pr0te tracks
104.9rally, 6 Sohaoying tracks
107.9after hours, Shut Down and Rainless (Hum), the player's own frequency

The broadcast counts from 1 January 2025 UTC. A song is usually already playing when you tune in, and two players on two computers stay within a second of each other.

Tape, scan, sleep

  • Tape plays the album in order. Side A is the 22 tracks of disc 1, side B the 6 tracks of disc 2.
  • Scan moves to the next station every 6 seconds.
  • Sleep runs 15, 30 or 60 minutes, fades out over the last 10 seconds and then pauses.
  • Keys 1 to 7 pick the stations, like preset buttons on a car radio.

Overlay on a game

Ctrl+Alt+O turns the window into a strip above all windows. The Ctrl+Alt keys work while a game has focus.

  • The black behind the lines is 0, 30, 60 or 100 % opaque, and the glow stays.
  • Mini mode is 320 by 64 pixels with the frequency, station, track and time.
  • Clicks pass through the strip into the game.
  • A tray icon with a menu and a settings window laid out like the game's settings.
  • A Discord status: "Listening to EDC Radio" with the track, the station and a time bar. It is on by default and can be turned off in the settings.

The scene at the bottom

While music plays, the truck drives along a night road at 10 to 34 pixels a second, depending on the station. From 18:00 to 7:00 the headlights are on and the moon and stars are out. The cat at the radio tower nods to the beat, and every 40 to 120 seconds the snow shifts between a few flakes and a blizzard.

Steam soundtrack or your folder

The player contains no music and does not search your disk for it. There are three sources:

  • the Steam soundtrack, app 3994280, found on its own when installed;
  • a folder you pick with the "folder" button;
  • the game's CustomRadio folder for station 88.1.

Formats are FLAC, WAV, M4A, MP3 and WMA. Without music the window offers to pick a folder or to open the soundtrack page on Steam.

If something breaks

  • A track that does not open is skipped, and its name shows in the status line.
  • On a crash the player saves its settings and writes a report to %APPDATA%\EdcRadio\crash.log.
  • A damaged settings.json is kept next to it as settings.json.bad, and the player starts with default settings.

Report a bug: the form asks for the version, Windows and where the music comes from. If there is a crash.log, attach it.

What it does not do

  • It contains no music and no game graphics. It takes the fonts and icons from the installed game and opens its files read-only.
  • It changes nothing in the game or Steam files.
  • The overlay does not show over games in exclusive fullscreen. It works with windowed and borderless games.
  • It is not an official program. The game's author and the composers have nothing to do with it.
